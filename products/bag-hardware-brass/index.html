Bag hardware

Solid brass bag hardware

Buckles, D-rings, snap hooks, rivets and slider adjusters in solid brass. Plated in nickel, antique brass or black, with your logo engraved or embossed.

Why brass

Brass is the material that ages well. Where zinc alloy eventually pits and flakes, solid brass develops a patina and keeps its structural strength. For a bag that is meant to be used for years rather than a season, it is the more honest choice.

We machine and finish in-house, which means the plating thickness and the edge finish are controlled rather than bought in. If you have had hardware go dull after three months, it is usually a plating problem — and it is the first thing we check with a new customer's existing sample.

Items in this range

  • Buckles — rectangular, rounded, roller and ladder types
  • D-rings and O-rings, welded or open
  • Snap hooks and swivel hooks
  • Slider adjusters and strap keepers
  • Rivets, studs and screw-back plates
  • Magnetic snaps and turn locks
