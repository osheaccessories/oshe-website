3D printing prototypes

Why buyers trust our samples

Once your drawing is confirmed, we print a 1:1 3D prototype before cutting any metal. Shape, proportions and assembly can be judged in hand — and revisions only change the print file, not the mould.

Why we print before tooling
  • Hold it first. A render and a real object are different things — proportions and hand feel need a physical check.
  • Zero tooling cost for revisions. Changes only touch the print file; iterate until you are satisfied, then tool up.
  • No rework after tooling. Structure and assembly are fully verified at the prototype stage.
  • Faster to market. A prototype is in your hands in 3–7 days, well ahead of any mould.
Customisation range

What we can customise

Material options

304 food-grade (our sample spoons carry the SUS304 stamp), 316 for salt and acid resistance, 430 for induction cooktops, 201 where cost matters more. Material certificates available.

Forming processes

Stamping, deep drawing, spinning, laser cutting, TIG and spot welding, beading — thick or thin stock, and tooling for irregular shapes.

Surface finishes

Mirror polish, brushed, sandblasted, electropolished, titanium plating in gold / black / rose gold, or matte anti-fingerprint coating.

Size customisation

From a thumb-size sauce dish to a Ø40 cm basin, scaled from your drawing. Tolerances held to your specification; critical dimensions inspected 100%.

Logo & packaging

Laser marking, etching or embossing; retail boxes, inner trays and instruction cards in your branding, photographed for approval before shipment.

Low minimums

100 pcs for standard items; tooling items quoted as tooling fee plus unit price. Sample fees are credited against the bulk order.
