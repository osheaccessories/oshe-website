The factory

Twelve years of making metal parts

We are a manufacturer in Dongguan, Guangdong, not a trading company. Production, assembly and inspection happen under one roof — which is why we can give you a lead time and mean it.

What happens here

Oshe has been producing bag hardware and zippers since 2014. The work we do in-house covers stamping, machining, polishing, plating coordination, assembly and final inspection. Zipper chain and tape are sourced from long-standing suppliers and finished here — we say this plainly rather than implying everything is extruded on site.

Because the assembly and inspection steps are ours, the thing we can control is the thing that most often goes wrong elsewhere: the last five percent. Plating thickness, edge burr, slider travel, colour consistency between batches.

How you can check us

  • Third-party inspection before shipment — SGS, Intertek, BV or your own representative
  • Live video walkthrough from the production floor, at any stage
  • Factory visit by appointment; we will not rush you through
  • Approved sample retained against your order as the bulk reference
  • Photos of your actual production run before packing, not stock images

Documents

We do not hold product certifications at this stage, and we do not display badges we have not been issued. When certificates are obtained they will be published here with their numbers, so that a buyer can verify them. Until then, ask us for whatever you need for your file and we will tell you honestly whether we can provide it.

If your compliance team requires a specific test before the first order, tell us at the enquiry stage — most common tests can be arranged at cost.

Working with us

What a first order looks like

01

You send the part

A drawing, a photo, or the physical sample you already use. Quantity and end use help us quote accurately.

02

We quote and sample

Price and lead time within 12 hours. Stock samples in 2–3 days, custom samples in 15 days.

03

You approve

Photos first, then the physical sample. We keep the approved piece as the reference for the bulk run.

04

We make and ship

30% deposit, 30 days production, inspection before packing, balance before dispatch, EXW or FOB.
